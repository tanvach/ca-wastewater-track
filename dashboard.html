Visualization Logic:
1. 7-Day Average: Smoothes out daily noise (rainfall/flow).
2. Normalization: 100% = The highest viral load seen in the last 12 months (or the selected time range if longer). This highlights seasonal spikes better by ignoring ancient outliers.
3. Exclusions: PMMoV (fecal marker) and non-respiratory targets are hidden by default.
